4. Grammar Cheat-Sheet (memorize)

  • Subject–Verb agreement: singular subject → singular verb. "The list of items is long."
  • Preposition pairs: at=point, in=enclosed, on=surface. since+point in time, for+duration. between(2), among(3+). beside=next to, besides=apart from.
  • Confused words: affect(verb)/effect(noun) · their/there/they're · its/it's · principal/principle · complement/compliment · stationary/stationery · council/counsel · elicit/illicit.
  • Absolute/comparative: "very unique", "more better", "most perfect" are ALWAYS wrong.
  • Articles: "an hour" (h silent), "a university" (y sound), "an apple", "a book".
  • Double negatives: "didn't do nothing" → "didn't do anything".
  • Tense consistency: keep same tense unless time words force a change.

6. Sample Verbal Questions (test yourself)

Choose the correct form: "Neither of the two students ___ present."

Answer: was (singular subject "neither").

Error spotting: "He has bought (a) / a new car (b) / last week (c) / No error (d)."

Answer: a — "has bought" (present perfect) can't be used with a specific past time "last week". Use "bought".

Synonym of "candid":

Answer: frank / straightforward.

Antonym of "benevolent":

Answer: malevolent / cruel / unkind.

Fill in blank: "She has been living here ___ 2019."

Answer: since (since + point in time).

Jumble opener: a) However, this proved false. b) Many believed the city was safe. c) Recent data shows rising crime. d) Officials urged caution.

Answer: b (introduces the belief) → a → c → d.
