4. Grammar Cheat-Sheet (memorize)

  • Subject–Verb agreement: singular → singular. "The list of items is long."
  • Prepositions: at=point, in=enclosed, on=surface. since+point, for+duration. between(2)/among(3+). beside=next to, besides=apart from.
  • Confused words: affect/effect · their/there/they're · its/it's · principal/principle · complement/compliment · stationary/stationery.
  • Never: "very unique", "more better", "most perfect".
  • Articles: an hour (h silent), a university (y sound), an apple, a book.
  • Double negatives: "didn't do nothing" → "didn't do anything".
  • Tense consistency unless time words force a change.

5. Sample Questions

"Neither of the two students ___ present."

was — singular subject "neither".

Error: "He has bought a new car last week."

"has bought" (present perfect) can't take a specific past time. Use "bought".

Synonym of "candid" / Antonym of "benevolent"?

frank / malevolent.

"She has lived here ___ 2019."

since (point in time).
